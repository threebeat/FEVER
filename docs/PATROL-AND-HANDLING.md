# FEVER · Patrol recordings and first-person handling

Reed is driving with Cole buckled into the front passenger seat when dispatch assigns a disturbance at the municipal animal-control facility. The two answer alone. Reed starts with a service pistol, a loaded 13-round magazine and four spare magazines. Landry arrives at a Louisiana house in a separate car from her partner. A Vermont officer takes the service road to a cabin at a ski resort outside Stowe. Each wears a different recorder and is accountable for what they report. The range offers the same controls as the recordings.

The target aim blend reaches 90 percent in about 89 ms. The input buffer is 120 ms. Both numbers are FEVER tuning. A trigger press is sampled independently of its held state, so a click between simulation updates survives one frame. The analog trigger has separate press and release thresholds to prevent a lightly held trigger from chattering. The range regression compares integrated stick rotation at 30, 60 and 144 render samples per second and checks a tap on either side of the buffer window. Test the same actions on the same plate with mouse and controller and with each rendering preset.

## Police-car camera and dispatch

Ohio's published body-worn frame 39509 shows the wheel central in the foreground, a console to the right, and a sleeve and mirror on the left. There is less uninterrupted windshield than a conventional driving game would show. Use an original 3D interior with nearby pillars, hands, wheel, dashboard reflections and passing streetlights. Start on black with BODYCAM ON and a microphone/buffer check. Open the feed mid-patrol. Advance the local timestamp across a short cut to arrival. Move the mount with the officer leaving the seat. [Released frame](https://ohioattorneygeneral.gov/getattachment/fbbd2db9-8721-43d4-b2a4-921587a3065e/fbbd2db9-8721-43d4-b2a4-921587a3065e.aspx)

Livingston County routes animal complaints through central dispatch and requests an incident address. St. Tammany Parish's public information asks for location, callback and the kind of help requested. The Town of Stowe describes its mountain-rescue service. Those sources anchor the calls. Port Mercer, Cypress Parish and Switchback Ridge are fictional. Record the officers' voices as dry mono takes; dispatch and the person standing beside the wearer take different paths through the game's mixer. [Livingston County](https://milivcounty.gov/animal-control/file-a-complaint/) · [St. Tammany Parish](https://www.stp911.org/dialing-9-1-1/) · [Town of Stowe](https://www.stowevt.gov/Departments/Mountain-Rescue)
